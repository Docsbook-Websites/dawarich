# Dawarich Features

Everything below works the same on a self-hosted instance and in the cloud.

<!-- widget:cards -->

## The map

- [Map](./map.md) — Explore your location history on interactive maps with points, routes, heatmaps, and multiple layer options {map}
- [Points](./points.md) — Browse, search, export, and manage your recorded GPS location points in Dawarich {circle-dot}
- [Areas](./areas.md) — Define geographic areas on the map to automatically track and log your visits in their boundaries {shapes}
- [Visits and Places](./visits-and-places.md) — Track your visits to locations and build a comprehensive picture of your daily activities and travel history {map-pin}

## Making sense of it

- [Insights](./insights.md) — Analyze your location history with visualizations, statistics, and movement pattern dashboards {lightbulb}
- [Stats](./stats.md) — View summary statistics of your location data including distance traveled, countries and cities visited, {chart-column}
- [Trips](./trips.md) — Create, organize, and visualize your trips with routes, dates, and notes in Dawarich {route}
- [Transportation Modes](./transportation-modes.md) — Automatically detect and classify your transportation modes based on speed and movement patterns {car}
- [Days per Country](./tax-residency.md) — Count the days you spent in each country from your location history, with consecutive stay periods and a {landmark}
- [Place Search](./search.md) — Search for places on the map and view your visit history for those locations {search}

## Photos and people

- [Photos](./photos.md) — Integrate Immich or Photoprism with Dawarich to display your photos on the map and in trips {image}
- [Enrich Immich Photos](./enrich-photos.md) — Write GPS coordinates from Dawarich to Immich photos that lack geodata {image-plus}
- [Family Location Sharing](./family.md) — Share your real-time location with trusted family members and friends on your self-hosted Dawarich instance {users}

## Data in and out

- [Imports](./imports.md) — View and manage your location data imports, including file names, point counts, and processing status {upload}
- [Exports](./exports.md) — Export your location data from Dawarich in JSON format for backup or use in other applications {download}
- [Tracking Location History](./tracking-location-history.md) — Set up location tracking with the native iOS or Android app, Overland, OwnTracks, Traccar, or GPSLogger {history}
- [PhotoTagger](../tools/phototagger.md) — Write Dawarich location data back into your photo files {tags}
